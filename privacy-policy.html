Privacy Policy

Naam Simar – Digital Naam Jaap
by SiyaRam Labs
Last Updated: August 17, 2026

SiyaRam Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Naam Simar – Digital Naam Jaap mobile application ("Naam Simar" or the "App").

We respect your privacy. Naam Simar is designed to provide a private devotional experience, with practice information primarily stored and processed locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Naam Simar.

Privacy at a glance
  • No account is required.
  • No third-party advertising is currently used.
  • No third-party analytics or behavioral tracking is currently used.
  • Your devotional practice information is stored locally on your device.
  • Backup and restore is initiated and controlled by you.

1. Information We Collect

Naam Simar does not currently collect personal information or transmit your devotional practice data to SiyaRam Labs or servers operated by us.

The App does not currently require you to create an account or provide information such as your name, phone number, postal address, or date of birth.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Naam Simar stores information locally on your device in order to provide its features. This may include:

  • Naam Jaap and chant counts
  • Current and completed mala progress
  • Daily mala goals
  • Practice history and statistics
  • Practice streak information
  • Selected mantra
  • Custom mantras
  • App preferences and settings
  • Language preferences
  • Reminder settings
  • Chanting and audio preferences

This information is used to provide the App's functionality and is not transmitted by Naam Simar to SiyaRam Labs.

3. Custom Mantras and Audio

Naam Simar allows you to create custom mantras and optionally associate audio with them.

Custom mantra information is stored locally on your device.

When you select an audio file, the App accesses the file you choose for the purpose of providing the custom mantra audio feature. Naam Simar does not upload this audio to SiyaRam Labs or servers operated by us.

4. Backup and Restore

Naam Simar provides a user-controlled backup and restore feature.

When you create a backup, the App creates backup data containing supported practice information, preferences, and custom mantra information. Where supported, custom mantra audio may also be included in the backup.

You choose where the backup file is saved using your device's file-selection interface. Naam Simar does not automatically upload these backups to a server operated by SiyaRam Labs.

If you independently choose a cloud storage provider or another third-party location when saving, copying, or sharing your backup, that provider's privacy practices may apply.

You are responsible for protecting backup files that you create.

5. Daily Reminders and Notifications

Naam Simar may provide optional daily reminders to help you maintain your devotional practice.

Reminder schedules and related preferences are handled on your device. Notification permission may be requested where required by your Android version.

The App may use Android system functionality to restore or reschedule applicable reminders after your device restarts. These reminder features are not used for advertising or tracking.

6. Advertising

Naam Simar does not currently display third-party advertisements.

The current version of the App does not use advertising services for behavioral advertising or advertising-based user profiling.

7. Analytics and Tracking

Naam Simar does not currently use third-party analytics or behavioral tracking services.

We do not currently use your devotional practice information to build advertising profiles or track you across other apps or services.

8. Internet and External Data Transmission

The current version of Naam Simar is designed primarily as an offline application.

Devotional practice information, history, statistics, custom mantras, and related settings are processed locally rather than being transmitted to a SiyaRam Labs server.

9. Permissions and Device Capabilities

Naam Simar may request or use Android permissions and system capabilities required to provide particular features.

Notifications
Used to display devotional practice reminders when you enable them.

Device restart information
Used to restore or reschedule applicable reminders after your device restarts.

User-selected files
When you select custom mantra audio or choose a location for backup or restore, Android's system file-selection interface may be used to allow you to select the relevant file or location.

Naam Simar does not currently request access to your contacts, precise location, SMS messages, call history, camera, or microphone for its core functionality.

10. Day/Night Theme and Localization

Naam Simar may store your theme, language, and other display preferences locally on your device.

These preferences are used to customize your experience in the App.

11. Devotional Content

Naam Simar provides devotional features and content such as Naam Jaap, mantras, Paath, Aarti, Vrat, Katha, and related spiritual material.

Your interaction with this content is not currently transmitted to SiyaRam Labs for advertising or analytics purposes.

12. Data Sharing and Sale

SiyaRam Labs does not currently sell your personal information.

Naam Simar does not currently share your devotional practice history, chant counts, custom mantras, or similar locally stored practice information with advertisers or analytics companies.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Practice information is stored locally on your device.

Depending on the information involved, you may be able to modify or remove it through App functionality.

Uninstalling Naam Simar generally removes the App's locally stored application data from the device, subject to Android operating system behavior and any backup files that you separately created and stored.

Backup files created by you are under your control and must be deleted separately from wherever you chose to save them.

14. Children's Privacy

Naam Simar is a general devotional application and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Because the current App does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect personal information through our servers, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through Naam Simar.

If the App's data practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

15. Security

We design Naam Simar to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of information.

However, no device, software application, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should take appropriate precautions to protect their devices and any backup files they create.

16. Third-Party Services

The current version of Naam Simar does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Your Android device and services you independently choose to use, such as a file-storage provider selected when saving a backup, may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Such services are outside the control of SiyaRam Labs.

17. Future Changes to App Features

Naam Simar may evolve over time.

If we introduce features that materially change how information is collected, transmitted, shared, or processed, such as cloud synchronization, analytics, or advertising, we will update this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures as required.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Naam Simar's features, legal requirements, or privacy practices change.

Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
