Privacy Policy

Naam Simar – Digital Naam Jaap
by SiyaRam Labs
Last Updated: August 29, 2026

SiyaRam Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Naam Simar – Digital Naam Jaap mobile application ("Naam Simar" or the "App").

We respect your privacy. Naam Simar is designed to provide a private devotional experience, with practice information primarily stored and processed locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Naam Simar.

Privacy at a glance
  • No account is required.
  • No third-party advertising is currently used.
  • Google Firebase Analytics is used for limited app-usage measurement.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics is used for crash, non-fatal error, and app stability diagnostics.
  • We do not use Firebase to send your mantra text, custom mantra content, chant history/counts, audio files, or backup contents.
  • Your devotional practice information is stored locally on your device.
  • Backup and restore is initiated and controlled by you.

1. Information We Collect

Naam Simar does not require you to create an account or provide information such as your name, phone number, postal address, or date of birth.

Your devotional practice information is primarily stored locally on your device. However, the App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may process limited app-usage, installation, device, application, crash, and diagnostic information as described below.

Through our own analytics and crash-reporting instrumentation, we intentionally avoid sending mantra text, custom mantra content, chant history or counts, audio files or file paths, backup contents or locations, reminder times, or other free-form devotional content to Firebase.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Naam Simar stores information locally on your device in order to provide its features. This may include:

  • Naam Jaap and chant counts
  • Current and completed mala progress
  • Daily mala goals
  • Practice history and statistics
  • Practice streak information
  • Selected mantra
  • Custom mantras
  • App preferences and settings
  • Language preferences
  • Reminder settings
  • Chanting and audio preferences

This information is used to provide the App's functionality and remains primarily on your device. The App does not intentionally send the contents of this locally stored devotional practice information to Firebase through our custom analytics or crash-reporting events.

3. Custom Mantras and Audio

Naam Simar allows you to create custom mantras and optionally associate audio with them.

Custom mantra information is stored locally on your device.

When you select an audio file, the App accesses the file you choose for the purpose of providing the custom mantra audio feature. Naam Simar does not upload this audio to SiyaRam Labs or servers operated by us.

4. Backup and Restore

Naam Simar provides a user-controlled backup and restore feature.

When you create a backup, the App creates backup data containing supported practice information, preferences, and custom mantra information. Where supported, custom mantra audio may also be included in the backup.

You choose where the backup file is saved using your device's file-selection interface. Naam Simar does not automatically upload these backups to a server operated by SiyaRam Labs.

If you independently choose a cloud storage provider or another third-party location when saving, copying, or sharing your backup, that provider's privacy practices may apply.

You are responsible for protecting backup files that you create.

5. Daily Reminders and Notifications

Naam Simar may provide optional daily reminders to help you maintain your devotional practice.

Reminder schedules and related preferences are handled on your device. Notification permission may be requested where required by your Android version.

The App may use Android system functionality to restore or reschedule applicable reminders after your device restarts. These reminder features are not used for advertising or tracking.

6. Advertising

Naam Simar does not currently display third-party advertisements.

The current version of the App does not use advertising services for behavioral advertising or advertising-based user profiling.

7. Analytics and App Usage Measurement

Naam Simar uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand limited, aggregate patterns of how App features are used and to help us improve the App.

The App may record selected product-usage events such as completion of onboarding, starting a Simran session, completing a mala, using auto chant, creating or deleting a custom mantra, changing selected settings, completing a user-initiated backup or restore, and opening categories of devotional content.

Parameters attached to these events are deliberately limited. For example, the App may record whether a mantra is built-in or custom, whether chanting is manual or automatic, whether custom audio is present, or the general type of devotional content opened.

We intentionally do not send mantra names or text, custom mantra content, individual chant taps, chant counts, audio file names or paths, reminder times, backup file locations or contents, or devotional text bodies as our custom Firebase Analytics parameters.

The Firebase Analytics SDK may also automatically process certain technical, app, device, installation, and usage information in accordance with Google's Firebase and Google Analytics practices. We do not use Naam Simar's Analytics implementation to build our own advertising profile from your devotional practice data or to sell your information.

9. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

Naam Simar uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes, non-fatal errors, Application Not Responding (ANR) events, and other stability problems.

Crashlytics may automatically process crash stack traces, relevant application state, relevant device metadata, installation identifiers, app version information, and other technical diagnostic information needed to provide crash-reporting and app-stability services.

Naam Simar may also send selected diagnostic breadcrumbs and developer-defined non-fatal error information to help us understand what occurred before a failure. Our logging policy is designed to avoid personal information, authentication secrets, mantra text, custom mantra content, audio paths, backup contents, and tap-by-tap chanting information.

9. Internet and External Data Transmission

Naam Simar remains designed primarily around locally stored devotional practice data and can provide its core chanting features without a SiyaRam Labs account or cloud account.

Limited technical, usage, crash, and diagnostic information may be transmitted to Google through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics when those services operate. This transmission is used for app-usage measurement, troubleshooting, crash reporting, and improving App reliability.

Devotional practice history, chant counts, custom mantra text, custom audio files, and backup contents are not intentionally uploaded to a SiyaRam Labs server or sent as our custom Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics data.

10. Permissions and Device Capabilities

Naam Simar may request or use Android permissions and system capabilities required to provide particular features.

Notifications
Used to display devotional practice reminders when you enable them.

Device restart information
Used to restore or reschedule applicable reminders after your device restarts.

User-selected files
When you select custom mantra audio or choose a location for backup or restore, Android's system file-selection interface may be used to allow you to select the relevant file or location.

Naam Simar does not currently request access to your contacts, precise location, SMS messages, call history, camera, or microphone for its core functionality.

11. Day/Night Theme and Localization

Naam Simar may store your theme, language, and other display preferences locally on your device.

These preferences are used to customize your experience in the App.

12. Devotional Content

Naam Simar provides devotional features and content such as Naam Jaap, mantras, Paath, Aarti, Vrat, Katha, and related spiritual material.

The App may record a general category of devotional content opened for product analytics. We do not intentionally send the devotional text body, your custom mantra text, or individual chanting content as our custom analytics parameters.

13. Data Sharing and Sale

SiyaRam Labs does not sell your personal information.

Limited technical, analytics, and diagnostic information is processed by Google as our third-party service provider through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for the purposes described in this Policy.

We do not intentionally share your locally stored devotional practice history, chant counts, custom mantra text, custom audio files, or backup contents with advertisers.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Practice information is stored locally on your device.

Depending on the information involved, you may be able to modify or remove it through App functionality.

Uninstalling Naam Simar generally removes the App's locally stored application data from the device, subject to Android operating system behavior and any backup files that you separately created and stored.

Backup files created by you are under your control and must be deleted separately from wherever you chose to save them.

Information processed by Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices. Removing the App from your device does not necessarily immediately delete information that was previously transmitted to and retained by those services.

15. Children's Privacy

Naam Simar is a general devotional application and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

The App does not require account registration and is not intended to ask children to provide names, contact details, or other free-form personal information. Limited technical, analytics, and diagnostic information may nevertheless be processed through Firebase as described in this Policy.

If the App's data practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

16. Security

We design Naam Simar to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of information.

However, no device, software application, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should take appropriate precautions to protect their devices and any backup files they create.

17. Third-Party Services

Naam Simar currently uses the following Google Firebase services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — used for limited app-usage measurement and product improvement.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — used for crash, non-fatal error, ANR, and app-stability diagnostics.

Information processed through these services is handled by Google according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Your Android device and services you independently choose to use, such as a file-storage provider selected when saving a backup, may also process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Such third-party services are outside the direct control of SiyaRam Labs.

18. Future Changes to App Features

Naam Simar may evolve over time.

If we introduce features that materially change how information is collected, transmitted, shared, or processed, such as cloud synchronization, advertising, or materially different analytics, we will update this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures as required.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Naam Simar's features, legal requirements, or privacy practices change.

Any revised policy will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
